## Figure provenance

- The eleven conceptual diagrams (nine main illustrations and two margin sketches) are original work generated by `tools/sketches.py`, called by `tools/figures.py`; matching `.excalidraw` scenes are editable. Arrows show conceptual dependencies, not measured overlap or durations. The expert grids, route excerpt and cache addresses are teaching examples rather than captured production tensors. Hatched fills indicate ownership or work categories, not measured utilization.
- The result chart is generated with Matplotlib from `results.csv`. Bars start at zero improvement. Separate candidate runs have no supplied candidate-comparison confidence intervals; no error bars or significance claims are invented.
- The tile explorer implements the pinned strict row-count thresholds. Same tile does not imply proven numerical equivalence.

## Reproduction boundaries

The static article and figures can be rebuilt locally. Reproducing GPU experiments requires the original project, pinned dependencies, appropriate hardware and model access; this repository does not pretend to provide them. There are no credentials, raw conversations or weights in the selected evidence pack.

The excerpts discuss AI-assisted research and implementation explicitly. No personal lecture-viewing history is fabricated. No claim is made that every SGLang file, every course or every public implementation was read.
